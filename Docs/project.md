# Project.md — TechRise Initiative

## What is this?
The official website for **TechRise Initiative**, a startup/organization focused on empowering individuals through technology, innovation, and global collaboration.

## Mission
> Empowering individuals through technology, innovation, and global collaboration.

TechRise Initiative helps learners evolve into creators by building real-world skills, engaging in meaningful competitions, and contributing to impactful projects. Through education, collaboration, and innovation, the goal is to make technology accessible to all and inspire the next generation of problem-solvers.

## Why this website exists
Currently, TechRise Initiative's presence is limited to social captions and DMs. The website centralizes:
- Who they are and what they do (public credibility)
- What programs/competitions/events are running (discoverability)
- A way for learners, volunteers, and sponsors to get involved (conversion)
- A way to showcase real projects built by the community (proof of impact)
- A way to accept donations to fund initiatives (sustainability)

## Target audience
- **Students/learners** — looking for skill-building programs, competitions, or projects to join
- **Volunteers/mentors** — want to contribute time/expertise
- **Sponsors/partners** — evaluating credibility before funding or partnering
- **General public** — discovering the initiative, reading updates, or donating

*(Site is designed for all four audiences — no single group is prioritized over another in navigation/IA.)*

## Core value proposition
1. **Learn** — access programs and competitions that build real skills
2. **Build** — contribute to and showcase real-world projects
3. **Connect** — collaborate globally with other learners, mentors, and partners
4. **Support** — donate or sponsor to keep the mission running

- A donor can complete a donation without confusion or errors
- Site is fully responsive and loads fast on mobile
